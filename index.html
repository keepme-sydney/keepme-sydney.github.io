Why this tour

Plans for Sydney Square require the removal of the block between George St and Pitt St. Seven buildings are to be demolished because they are not heritage listed.

No proper assessment has been made of their value. The current model discards embodied carbon, material resources and existing building capacity in the pursuit of the new.

We are advocating for an architectural practice that works within existing planning and development structures to change how redevelopment decisions are made. This practice proposes that existing buildings should first be assessed for their capacity to be retained and adapted before demolition and redevelopment are considered.
